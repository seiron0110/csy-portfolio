# Scaling an REI SaaS to 5-Figure MRR

Real Estate Investing · SaaS

SaaS Platform

## Summary

Rebuilt sales, onboarding, and fulfillment inside GoHighLevel. Scaled from ~40–50 to 100+ clients and 5-figure MRR.

## Results

| Result | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Clients served | 100+ |
| Revenue | 5-figure MRR |
| Onboarding time | 15–30 min |

## Business outcome

An early-traction REI CRM was winning clients faster than operations could deliver. Sales, onboarding, and fulfillment lived in disconnected steps, so growth created chaos instead of momentum.

A rebuilt delivery system made onboarding fast, fulfillment repeatable, and support a retention advantage, so the business could scale clients and revenue without breaking day-to-day ops.
